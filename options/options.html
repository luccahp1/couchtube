Browsing

  • D-pad move focus
  • A open
  • B back
  • X search keyboard
  • Y couch zoom
  • LB / RB page up / down
  • View jump to the player

Player

  • A play / pause
  • D-pad ←→ seek, hold to scrub
  • D-pad ↑↓ volume
  • X captions
  • Y fullscreen
  • LT / RT speed
  • B leave fullscreen, then browse
